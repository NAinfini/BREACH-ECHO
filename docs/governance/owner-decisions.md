# Only the decisions that need you / 仅需所有者裁决

You do not need to choose networking, programming patterns, save formats, routine balancing, folder names or every piece of equipment. Those choices are delegated and recorded. The items below remain yours because they determine the game's identity, spend your money, require your accounts, or change something you explicitly required. None prevents starting a no-purchase graybox.

## OWNER-01 — Story and four characters

**Your decision:** approve or revise the complete [story overview](../gdd/story-overview.md), including the proposed connecting history, the four codenames `断桥 / 回声 / 铁砧 / 寒蝉`, their personalities and relationships. Explicitly approved historical facts remain canon regardless of this review. **Recommendation:** approve the world structure for writing; judge the codenames separately rather than approving names merely to unblock technical work.

**Why yours:** character identity and authorial meaning are creative ownership, not a library choice. Earlier instructions explicitly reserved this review and postponed age, body, appearance and casting. **Until then:** graybox Seat 01–04 and neutral tactical callouts; no irreversible character art or paid voice sessions. After your review, commission a genuinely fresh read-only story review with no prior project context. No such blind review has been performed in this documentation pass.

## OWNER-02 — Money, accounts, commercial identity and production capacity

**Your decision:** authorize a maximum initial asset/tool budget, maximum recurring service budget, and any paid specialist work; later approve final store name, price and release date. Provide account access only through appropriate secure connections, never pasted credentials. **Recommendation now:** buy nothing until M1 shows the core interaction and a specific purchase removes a measured bottleneck. Authorize any multiplayer coordinator deployment only after receiving its limits, forecast, alerts and disable procedure.

**Why yours:** documentation cannot bind you to expenditure, licensing terms, a cloud bill, a legal entity or a public release. Unity/Steam/Meshy/vendor terms and actual asset licenses must be checked for the account and purchase involved. This public Git repository must not receive purchased raw models or confidential partner material. **Until then:** local tooling, original graybox assets, mocked platform/control-plane tests; real online service, Steam partner activation and commercial clearance remain deployment gates. Available weekly effort and staffing are unknown, so the roadmap is milestone-based rather than a promised calendar.

## OWNER-03 — Final visual and voice identity

**Your decision:** accept a concrete in-engine visual target and the characters' appearance/voice direction after personality approval. **Recommendation:** stylized industrial realism, strong silhouettes, readable industrial materials and restrained effects; use purchased generic environment assets only where they fit the same visual language, and invest original work in characters, signature threats and key facilities.

**Why yours:** a written style label is not the same as liking the actual game on screen. **Until then:** neutral graybox proportions and a small environment look test; no invented ages, genders, faces, body types or casting. Technical import rules, Blender cleanup, rig QA and asset provenance are already chosen in the asset pipeline and do not require you to choose tools.

## OWNER-04 — Any reduction of an explicit requirement

**Your decision only when evidence demands it:** whether to remove or alter an explicitly required feature such as optional TPS, optional bots, Steam Deck 60 FPS, host migration, detailed statistics, temporary local replay, fixed canon characters, or safe native mod synchronization. **Recommendation:** retain the contracts but prove them in dependency order; bring you a scoped tradeoff only after profiling/prototyping identifies the cost. This is a change-control rule, not a request for you to decide all these features again.

## What is not waiting for you

Operation-only base game; two guns/one tool/free tactical module; finite energy ammunition; no Operation auto-Fusion; Unity 6.3 LTS+URP; FishNet/Steam networking; the small online authority coordinator; snapshot recovery and bounded rewind; JSON authoring plus versioned binary runtime records; Data/Graph-only public mods initially; no downloaded DLLs; exact-hash cache policy; one integrated Mod Manager; Chinese and English text baseline; the staged implementation plan. Tests may overturn an implementation detail, but “not yet tested” does not mean “ask the owner to pick a technology.”
